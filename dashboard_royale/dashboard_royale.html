Method and data

How the numbers are built

Sources. Machine meters (machine_day, all play), game math (game_math), placements and zones (prepared_daily_view), tickets (operations), floor totals (property_day), tracked players (player_period), loyalty events (loyalty_ledger), walkways, action catalogue. Release 0.9.0-draft.

Money. House win = coin-in − payout. Theo = Σ bet × (1 − RTP). All ratios are ratios of sums. Off-floor days (machine not installed or retired) are excluded, not counted as zero.

Luck. For each machine, the standard deviation of house win is √Σ(payout-multiplier variance × bet²), from published game variance and actual bet sizes. Luck z = (house win − theo) ÷ that SD. Hit-rate z = (paying spins − spins × p) ÷ √(spins × p × (1 − p)).

Exposure. Comparisons use theo per available hour (scheduled hours minus downtime; DST day has 23 h) so new, retired or broken machines are not penalized for missing time.

Model. Train Jan 1 – Apr 30, test May 1 – Jun 30. Baseline = training-period mean daily coin-in. Model = training mean for each weekday. Metric = mean absolute error in dollars.

Limitations. Synthetic data. Carded play only for guests. Downtime cost assumes normal demand while down. Action benefits are estimates with stated ranges, not guarantees. An unusual payout pattern is a reason to inspect, not evidence of wrongdoing.
